The problem

Generic POS software was never made for a hardware store.

Most systems assume you sell a handful of neat products. A hardware store sells bolts by the piece and by the box, cement by the bag, wire by the meter — to walk-ins and to contractors who pay later. Built4U was built around that reality.

BUILT4U

What’s inside

One system, from the counter to the back office.

Everything here is included — not modules you unlock later.

01 / Point of sale

Fast enough for a queue

Barcode scanning, split payments, held sales, and price overrides that need a manager’s approval — built for speed at a busy counter.

02 / Inventory

One count, always current

Thousands of items across categories, locations and units. Every delivery, transfer, sale and adjustment updates the same figure, with an audit trail behind it.

03 / Customer credit

Utang, with a paper trail

Track what each customer owes, take partial payments, and see aging so nothing quietly goes uncollected. Supplier payables work the same way.

04 / Shift & cash control

You know if the drawer is short

Cashiers open and close a shift with a denomination count, and cash taken in or out mid-shift is recorded against a name.

05 / Purchasing & receiving

From order to shelf

Purchase orders with approval routing, deliveries received against the PO, and costs updated as the stock actually arrives.

06 / Reports

Questions that get answers

Profit and margin, sales by cashier or by hour, dead stock, reorder suggestions, inventory valuation — on screen, exported, or emailed on a schedule.

Also goods receiving · stock transfers between branches · stocktake · supplier records · customer purchase history · vouchers and loyalty points · multi-branch · roles and permissions · full audit log · bulk item import · automatic cloud backup

Questions

Fair questions before you commit.

Something specific to your store? Ask us — we’d rather answer it with your own items on the screen.

Do I need to buy new hardware?

Usually not. Built4U runs in a web browser on a normal PC or laptop, and works with standard USB barcode scanners and network thermal printers. If you already have a counter setup, it most likely works as is.

What happens if the internet goes down?

Being straight with you: the system needs a connection, so a long outage stops sales. Most stores keep a mobile hotspot as backup. Offline mode is on the roadmap — ask us where it stands.

Can I bring my existing data in?

Yes. Send whatever you have — spreadsheets, exports, or lists — and we handle the import, including cleaning up duplicates and inconsistent units.

Who can see what?

You decide. Cashiers can be limited to selling, while discounts, stock adjustments, cost prices and reports stay with you. Sensitive actions are recorded against the staff member’s name.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many branches you run and how much setup help you need. Message us and we’ll quote you plainly — no lock-in contract.
