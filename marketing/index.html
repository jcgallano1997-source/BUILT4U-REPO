The problem

Generic POS software was never made for a hardware store.

Most systems assume you sell a handful of neat products. A hardware store sells bolts by the piece and by the box, cement by the bag, wire by the meter — to walk-ins and to contractors who pay later. Built4U was built around that reality.

✓Buy by box, sell by piece. Receive a box of 100 and the system converts it to pieces automatically — no mental math at the counter.
✓Thousands of SKUs, found fast. Search by code, name or barcode. Scan an item and it drops straight into the cart.
✓Customers on utang. Track credit per customer, accept partial payments, and see at a glance who owes what.
✓Real cost, not guesswork. Moving-average costing updates as supplier prices rise, so your margins are honest.
✓Deliveries, handled. Raise a purchase order, receive against it, and stock and costs update themselves.
✓Cash that reconciles. Open and close each shift with a denomination count, so you know if the drawer is short.

Questions

Fair questions before you commit.

Do I need to buy new hardware?

Usually not. Built4U runs in a web browser on a normal PC or laptop, and works with standard USB barcode scanners and network thermal printers. If you already have a counter setup, it most likely works as is.

What happens if the internet goes down?

Being straight with you: the system needs a connection, so a long outage stops sales. Most stores keep a mobile hotspot as backup. Offline mode is on the roadmap — ask us where it stands.

Can I bring my existing data in?

Yes. Send whatever you have — spreadsheets, exports, or lists — and we handle the import, including cleaning up duplicates and inconsistent units.

Is it BIR compliant?

Not yet, and we won’t pretend otherwise. Built4U currently prints a sales slip, not a BIR-accredited official receipt. VAT breakdown, senior and PWD discounts, and Z-readings are in active development — talk to us about your timeline.

Who can see what?

You decide. Cashiers can be limited to selling, while discounts, stock adjustments, cost prices and reports stay with you. Sensitive actions are recorded against the staff member’s name.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many branches you run and how much setup help you need. Request a demo and we’ll quote you plainly — no lock-in contract.
